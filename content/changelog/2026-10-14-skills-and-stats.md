## Skill points and masteries

- **More skill points:** every **150** SP-EXP now becomes a skill point (it was 400). Kills and quests give the same SP-EXP as before, so you earn SP almost three times as fast. A typical player reaches level 25 with about **8,500 SP**.
- **Already playing?** At your next login you get, once, the SP you would have earned so far at the new rate, and your SP-EXP bar turns into SP at the new rate too. Nothing you already learned changes.
- **The mastery total:** you can hold **4 mastery levels per character level** in total, so **100 at level 25**. That is enough to max **one weapon and Cold, Fire and Lightning** to 25, with every skill in them. Want Force as well? Then one of the elements has to stay lower.
- The skill window shows your total against the limit (for example **87 / 100**), and the tooltip says why a mastery cannot go up.
- If your masteries are already above the limit you keep them all; you just cannot raise one until you are under it.

## STR, INT and your gear

- **STR and INT count through your gear, like in the original game.** Your weapon adds a percentage of your STR to its physical attack and a percentage of your INT to its magical attack. Every armour piece, garment, protector and shield adds a percentage of your STR to its physical defence and of your INT to its magical defence. Better gear has higher percentages.
- Pure STR characters hit harder and take less physical damage, pure INT characters get more magic attack and magic defence, and hybrids get some of each. Bare hands keep the old small bonus.
- **The item tooltip shows the percentages**, for example "Phy. reinforce 52.8 % ~ 60.7 % (of STR)".
- **Broken gear no longer counts towards a set bonus:** a piece at 0 durability gives no stats, and now it does not count for a degree or seal set either. Repair it to get the bonus back.
- Thieves hit harder with the new STR and INT rule, so their damage against transports was lowered a little to keep robberies about as long as before.
