## Level 25

The level cap goes from **20 to 25**. If you were level 20, your EXP bar starts again at 0 % towards 21. Everyone else keeps their level, their place in the EXP bar, their SP, skills and items. Nothing is lost.

The road to 25 is long on purpose: about 26 hours of play to reach 20, and about as much again from 20 to 25.

## New monster levels

Every area now has its own level range, and the monsters match it:

- **The Millet Fields** (Grassland, Hill of Ye): 1 to 5
- **Lake, Swamp and the Old Graves**: 5 to 10
- **Yeoha's Forest** and the Robbers' Camp: 9 to 13
- **The Tomb Approach**: 12 to 15. The packs at its edge no longer attack newcomers on sight.
- **The Tiger Mountains** and the Bandit Stronghold: 14 to 19
- **The Ferry Heights** (the old ferry landing and the ridge above it): 19 to 23, and **the Sea Cliffs** (the island's west rim, high over the sea): 22 to 25. The Western China side is gone under the sea, so its monsters now haunt Jangan's own heights.

Some monsters have new names where the same kind lives at two levels: Grave Stone Ghost, Tomb Sentinel, Tomb Keeper, Restless Tomb Stone, Elder Yeoha, Stronghold Archer, Hyungno Shaman and Powder Ghost.

## Hunt at your own level

Monsters **2 or more levels below you** give less EXP: 15 % less for each level from the second, down to 10 %. Monsters **above you** give up to 15 % more. Your own level is now the best place to hunt.

## Gear and skills

- **Degree 4** is the new top gear (levels 21–25): weapons, shields, armour and accessories, with their Seals. It drops on the Ferry Heights and the Sea Cliffs and from the level-25 bosses. It is never sold.
- Every degree now fits inside the cap: degree 1 at levels 1–8, degree 2 at 8–15, degree 3 at 15–21, degree 4 at 21–25. A Seal needs the level of its grade (Star A, Moon B, Sun C).
- The **4th Lucky Powder** is sold at the alchemy shop for degree-4 gear.
- Masteries go to **25**, with the new skills of mastery levels 21 to 25.
- **Seal of Moon** and **Seal of Sun** degree-4 weapons come only from level-25 monsters and bosses. Lower monsters drop a Seal of Star instead. Degrees 1–3 drop all three Seals at the normal rates.

## The bosses at level 25

- **Tiger Girl** is now level 25: more HP, harder hits, and her fury triples her damage after 10 minutes. Bring a party of four. Her White Tigers are replaced by **Tiger Girl's Guards** (level 24). She drops degree-4 gear and can drop degree-4 Seals of Moon and Sun.
- The **Ice Yeti** is level 25 too (78,000 HP), and she can drop a degree-4 piece. The siege's **Bandit Warlord** is tougher.
- To steer Tiger Girl in Play the Boss you now need level 25. Downs count from level 20.
