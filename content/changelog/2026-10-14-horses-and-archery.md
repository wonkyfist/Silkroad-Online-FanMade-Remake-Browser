## Horses

- **Sit properly:** riders now sit astride the saddle instead of crouching on the horse's back.
- **Get off and it follows:** when you dismount, your horse **follows you like a pet**. It walks when you're close and runs to catch up. Click it to **get back on** at any time.
- **Send home or release:** **Send home** puts the horse away and keeps it for next time, with the same HP. **Release** lets it go for good, and its item is gone too. If your horse dies, its item is gone as well.
- **Mounted archery:** with a **bow** you can attack and use your bow skills from the saddle. Element imbues work too. Melee weapons and other skills still need you on foot. Your upper body draws and shoots while the horse keeps running under you, and you aim up or down at your target.
- The horse summon effect no longer loops forever.

## Bows and arrows

- **Bows shoot arrows.** Every bow attack uses **one arrow** from the stack in your shield slot. Run out and you can't shoot until you equip more. Archers made before this update get one free stack of arrows at their next login.
- **Arrow combos fly every arrow:** 2, 3 and 4 Arrow Combo now fire 2, 3 and 4 arrows, each on its own release. Each arrow does its damage when it hits, not all at once.
- Knockback skills now **push** the target back smoothly.
- Spears and glaives attack at the retail speed again, and so do bows.

## Ghost Walk - Phantom

- **Press it and you dash straight ahead**, no need to click the ground. Or aim it: click a spot or a target first and you dash there, up to the skill's reach.
- The lightning trail follows you for the whole dash.
- Keep holding a movement key and you **keep walking** when the dash ends. You don't have to press it again.

## Walking

- Press **R**, or use the **Walk / Run** slot in the Action window, to **walk** instead of run. The game remembers your choice.
- Walking is a **fixed pace**: speed scrolls and buffs only make you **run** faster. Slowing effects still slow you while walking.

## Fighting

- **Click a monster once to select it, double-click to attack.**
- **Every skill sounds:** each skill now plays a sound when you cast it and when it hits.
- Area skills hit every monster in range, and the players you're allowed to fight.
- A full check of every learnable skill fixed several that did nothing. **Vital Spot** now applies its weakening effects and **Harmony Therapy** works.
- Imbues glow softly at the grip instead of lighting up your whole body in white.

## Star, Moon and Sun weapons

- Sealed weapons keep the **natural look** of the weapon. The rarity now shows on the **edges**: living veins of light, a core that breathes, and a soft glow around the weapon's outline. Upgrades (+N) no longer wash the weapon out.
- **Weapon cloth moves:** tassels, ribbons and banners on weapons now sway in the wind, ripple as you run and swing, and react to storms.

## Registration limits

To keep the game fair, the server can now limit how many **accounts** and **characters** one person creates in a set time, and it can stop sign-ups from VPNs and hosting networks. If you play together on one home connection and get blocked, ask a GM to add you to the allowlist.

## For GMs

The GM window has a new **Toolbox** tab: one-click gear sets, starter kits, inventory tools, moderation, boosts and events, with search, favourites, recent commands and a command line with autocomplete.
